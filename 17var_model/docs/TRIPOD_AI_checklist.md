Abbreviations: AUPRC, area under the precision-recall curve; AUROC, area under the receiver operating characteristic curve; BMI, body mass index; CI, confidence interval; CITL, calibration-in-the-large; DPQ, depression screener module (NHANES); ECE, expected calibration error; KCHS, Korea Community Health Survey; KDCA, Korea Disease Control and Prevention Agency; KNHANES, Korea National Health and Nutrition Examination Survey; LightGBM, light gradient-boosting machine; MEC, mobile examination center; ML, machine learning; NCHS, National Center for Health Statistics; NHANES, National Health and Nutrition Examination Survey; PHQ-9, Patient Health Questionnaire-9; PROBAST, Prediction model Risk Of Bias Assessment Tool; SHAP, Shapley additive explanations; TRIPOD+AI, Transparent Reporting of a multivariable prediction model for Individual Prognosis Or Diagnosis, artificial intelligence extension.

| Missing data | 11 | D;E | Describe how missing data were handled. | Analytical methods | 12a | D | Describe how the data were used (eg, for development and evaluation of model performance) in the analysis, including whether the data were partitioned, considering any sample size requirements | Methods, Study Design and Data Sources and Model Development and Evaluation: 2,048,510 KCHS records split at cluster level into a 500,000-record training set and a 100,001-record hold-out sharing no cluster; no a priori sample-size calculation, ~18,000 training events reported instead. |
| Analytical methods | 12b | D | Depending on the type of model, describe how predictors were handled in the analyses (functional form, rescaling, transformation, or any standardisation) | Table 1 (age top-coded at 80 years) and Methods, Predictors and Harmonization (income recomputed as a within-cohort rank quintile) and Model Development and Evaluation (main-effects representation selected over four candidate interaction terms); ordinal scores modeled as numeric and standardized, and continuous predictors median-imputed, in Multimedia Appendix 1. |
| Analytical methods | 12c | D | Specify the type of model, rationale, all model building steps, including any hyperparameter tuning, and method for internal validation | Methods, Model Development and Evaluation: elastic net, random forest and LightGBM; 24-configuration randomized search plus identical 5-fold cross-validated grids inside the training set; pre-specified AUROC then calibration-slope tie-break selected LightGBM; internal validation in the cluster-disjoint hold-out. |

| Analytical methods | 12e | D;E | Specify all measures and plots used (and their rationale) to evaluate model performance (eg, discrimination, calibration, clinical utility) and, if relevant, to compare multiple models | Methods, Model Development and Evaluation: weighted AUROC, AUPRC, calibration slope, CITL, Brier, ECE, operating characteristics and decision-curve net benefit over thresholds 0.02-0.30; calibration assessed at the moderate level of the calibration hierarchy; ROC, calibration and decision-curve plots. |
| Analytical methods | 12f | E | Describe any model updating (eg, recalibration) arising from the model evaluation, either overall or for particular sociodemographic groups or settings | Methods, External Validation and Sensitivity Analyses: three out-of-sample updates cross-fitted over five PSU-grouped folds (intercept only; intercept plus slope; temperature scaling), applied to each external cohort overall without re-estimating predictor effects; intercept-plus-slope is the recommended update. |
| Analytical methods | 12g | E | For model evaluation, describe how the model predictions were calculated (eg, formula, code, object, application programming interface) | Methods, External Validation and Sensitivity Analyses and Data Availability: the KCHS-trained model scored NHANES without refitting through the development-fitted pipeline; fixed seed and hyperparameters, preprocessing specification, model card and code available from the corresponding author on request. |
| Class imbalance | 13 | D;E | If class imbalance methods were used, state why and how this was done, and any subsequent methods to recalibrate the model or the model predictions | Methods, Model Development and Evaluation: no class weighting, resampling or other imbalance correction; the learner was fitted on the natural 3.6% event rate, a departure from the plan documented in Table S2 in Multimedia Appendix 1. |
| Fairness | 14 | D;E | Describe any approaches that were used to address model fairness and their rationale | Methods, External Validation and Sensitivity Analyses: weighted subgroup AUROC and calibration slope across sex, age, education, household income quintile and, in NHANES, race and ethnicity, with the rationale stated and a 0.05 AUROC deviation from the cohort value pre-specified as the flag; results in Results, Interpretability, Fairness, and Clinical Utility and Table S14. |
